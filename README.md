# 🕵️ The Sus Files

Welcome to **The Sus Files**! This is a highly classified, chaotic archive designed exclusively to document your friends' most unhinged quotes, photos, and completely out-of-context moments. 

It's essentially a digital burn book meets an FBI evidence board.

## 📂 What is this?
Have a group chat where people say things that make you question their sanity? Do you have cursed images of your friends that deserve to be documented in a court of law? 

**The Sus Files** is where you store them. It's a collaborative scrapbook where you and your friends can upload evidence of each other being weird, tag yourselves, and then roast each other.

## 🔍 How it Works

### 1. Identify Suspects (The Crew)
First, you add your friends to the archive. They become "Suspects". Every suspect gets their own unique alias (Call Sign) and a signature color so you can easily track their crimes across the platform.

### 2. Compile Evidence (The Wall)
This is where the chaos lives. Anyone can upload evidence. Evidence can be:
- 💬 Out-of-context quotes
- 📸 Cursed or highly questionable images
- 🎵 Weird voice notes or audio clips
- 🎥 Questionable video clips
- 🗣️ Entire text conversations

### 3. Connect the Dots
Every piece of evidence on The Wall is tagged with the Suspects involved. 
You can view the evidence in a standard grid, or switch to **Tree Mode** — where the app literally draws red string between your friends and their associated crimes, just like a conspiracy board!

### 4. Roast & React
Users can leave emoji reactions on the evidence. The post with the most reactions is crowned the 🔥 **Roast of the Day** and gets pinned to the top of the board.
